# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Individuals, couples, and families in New York seeking psychotherapy. They are often navigating sensitive issues like LGBTQ+ concerns, depression, trauma, or relationship conflict, and need a secure, affirming environment.

## Product Purpose
To showcase James Vining's psychotherapy services, build trust, list specialties, explain fees and insurance, and provide a clear contact form to schedule consultations.

## Brand Personality
Warm, professional, collaborative, and affirming. The styling (cream backgrounds, terracotta/slate accents) and type (Lora serif body text) evoke an editorial, thoughtful, and human-centric feel.

## Anti-references
Generic sterile corporate landing pages, bright neon SaaS templates, hyper-modern minimalist tech styles, and flat monochrome layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Safe and Welcoming Presence**: Use soft, natural tones and responsive spacing to make visitors feel safe and respected.
- **Relational Clarity**: Structure information (especially specialties, fees, and location) clearly and transparently to reduce friction and anxiety.
- **Affirming and Inclusive Design**: Maintain high standards of accessibility and language that affirms diverse identities (LGBTQ+, neurodivergence, etc.).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA level compliance. Keyboard navigation with skip links, aria attributes, contrast-resilient text, and system theme respects (reduced motion support).
